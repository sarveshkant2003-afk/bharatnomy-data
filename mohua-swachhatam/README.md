# Municipal solid waste generated and processed by state, Dec 2023

Tonnes of municipal solid waste generated per day in each state/UT's cities and towns, and how much of it is processed,
as reported by urban local bodies under Swachh Bharat Mission (Urban).

## Source

- **Data:** Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), reports by urban local bodies on the Swachhatam portal
- **Table taken from:** Lok Sabha Unstarred Question No. 3198 "Waste Processing Rates", answered 21 Dec 2023 (Annexure)
- **Link:** https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2972782/1/AU3198.pdf
- **Downloaded:** 19 Oct 2026

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `state_ut` | State or Union Territory |
| `waste_generated_tpd` | Municipal solid waste generated, tonnes per day |
| `waste_processed_tpd` | Municipal solid waste processed, tonnes per day |

## Notes

- Urban areas only. Lakshadweep is not in the source table, and there is no All India row.
- Nationally the reply gives about 1.5 lakh tonnes/day generated, 76.49% processed.
