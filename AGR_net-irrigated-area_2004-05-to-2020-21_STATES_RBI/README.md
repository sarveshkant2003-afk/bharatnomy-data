# Net irrigated area by state, 2004-05 to 2020-21

Land irrigated in a year, counting land irrigated more than once only once.

## Source

- **Data:** MoA&FW, Land Use Statistics
- **Table taken from:** RBI Handbook of Statistics on Indian States 2022-23, Table 58 "State-wise Pattern of Land Use - Net Irrigated Area"
- **Link:** https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=22122
- **Downloaded:** 19 Oct 2026

## Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `state_ut` | State or Union Territory. `All India` = national total |
| `year` | Agricultural year (July to June) |
| `net_irrigated_area_000_ha` | Net irrigated area, thousand hectares |
| `source_flag` | `estimated`, `provisional`, or `not_applicable` (state did not exist) |
